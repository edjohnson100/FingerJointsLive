How Gap Between Fingers & Gap To Part work

Gap Between Fingers also doubles as kerf compensation: a negative value oversizes the joint before cutting so the laser's kerf brings it back to a snug fit. The right value depends on your laser and material — start near your kerf width and adjust based on a test cut. Gap To Part is unrelated (a standoff gap, not kerf) and does not accept negative values.

Quick Help

  • 1st Body/Bodies: Select one or multiple bodies where the fingers will be placed.
  • 2nd Body/Bodies: Select one or multiple bodies where the notches will be placed.
  • Direction: Select an edge to define the joint's orientation. Leave this empty to automatically use the longest overlapping edge.

Reset to Defaults?

This resets all joint parameters to their factory defaults. Saved presets and your current body/direction selections are not affected.
